Hilal and his family

Hilal is the son of Zahra Atef Ahmed Wahdan, who is working hard to provide her family with safer and more stable living conditions.

After their home was completely destroyed, Zahra and her family were displaced from northern Gaza. They now live in a worn-out tent that offers limited protection from harsh weather. Rodents around the tent shelters present an additional challenge, highlighting the family’s need for safer and more hygienic living conditions.

Hilal, 26

Lives with physical, hearing, and visual disabilities. He needs essential equipment and supplies for his daily care.

Two daughters

School-aged, and trying to keep learning.

A son

Preparing for his Tawjihi examinations, hoping to continue his education despite displacement.

Essential needs of Zahra’s family

Hilal’s care

  • A wheelchair and a medical bed for Hilal
  • Adult diapers, medical supplies, and specialized nutritional products

Shelter and warmth

  • Winter clothing, mattresses, and blankets
  • A new, safer tent for better protection from cold weather and rain, and less exposure to rodents

Education

  • School supplies to help the children continue their education
